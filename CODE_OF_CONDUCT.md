# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

**Reporting.** To report behaviour that breaks this code, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting ("Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab) and state that it is a conduct report, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code.
